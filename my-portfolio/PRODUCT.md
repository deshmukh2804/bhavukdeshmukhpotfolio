# Creative developer portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A single-page creative-developer portfolio built to the user's supplied DESIGN.md. Work leads the experience, followed by capabilities, process, client feedback, profile, expertise, and contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The supplied workspace was empty. React, TypeScript, and Vite are the provisional stack proposed to the user; no existing framework, routes, or business logic was present. No site is published by this task.

## Brand Commitments
Near-black, subtle technical grid, off-white, medium green accent. Large centered typography, portfolio-first visual storytelling, restrained depth, Canvas smoke cursor, and accessible scroll motion. The provided DESIGN.md remains the visual source of truth.

## Evidence on Hand
The user-provided design brief is saved as DESIGN.md. Personal name, projects, images, testimonials, certificates, and contact information were requested and have not been provided. Preview project assets are original authored SVG concepts, explicitly marked as demo content. Do not turn demo content into claims about the owner. Testimonials and certificates support real data but default to honest empty states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive layout, semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, native pointer preserved, functional mobile navigation, reduced-motion behavior, and decorative animations that do not convey required information.
